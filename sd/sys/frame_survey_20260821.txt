1. WHAT IS ACTUALLY WRONG
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
a) RIGHT Y RAIL LOW AT REAR - shimmable, and the only mechanical fault worth
   correcting. Rail differential front-to-rear:
        cold 23C   0.344 mm      45C   0.372 mm      60C   0.358 mm
   TEMPERATURE INDEPENDENT to +/-0.02mm across 23K. Measure it cold.
   -> shim schedule, section 3.

b) X BEAM BOWS WITH TEMPERATURE - not correctable, mesh it.
   -11.7 microns/K, LINEAR in frame dT (verified at 3 temperatures: the change
   at 45C is 0.597 of the total to 60C, where linear predicts 0.571).
   Cause: steel MGN9 rail on a 20x20x3 6063 beam behaves as a bimetallic strip.
   A shim is a constant and this is not - it cannot be corrected mechanically.
   The real fix is the dual MGN9 sandwich already on the project list: symmetry
   cancels the couple, and it also addresses the 215Hz torsional mode.

c) Z DATUM MOVES A LOT WITH TEMPERATURE.
        cold -> 45C   +0.511 mm       cold -> 60C   +0.782 mm
   Linear in chamber dT to ~1%. Harmless IF you re-datum hot; fatal if you home
   cold and print hot. Print start MUST re-datum once soaked.

d) THE BED IS NOT THE PROBLEM. It rests freely on three points, so it can only
   contribute a PLANE, and G32 nulls a plane at every print. Its mounting needs
   no work.

e) THE FRAME NEEDS NO LEVELLING. 4.35mm of shim under one foot moved the twist
   by 6 microns (0.0014 mm per mm). Machine position on the bench is not a
   variable. Do not buy levelling feet or a stand.

f) SURFACE ERRORS, for information - the mesh handles these, no action:
        granite  0.114 mm astigmatic (it is NOT a flat reference)
        FR4      0.028 mm astigmatic
   The FR4's warp happens to oppose the granite's and cancels much of it. Its
   CURRENT ORIENTATION IS THE GOOD ONE - 0.395mm range vs 0.635mm rotated 90.
   Mark it before ever lifting it.

g) REPEATABILITY: 4.7 microns cold, 8-10 microns hot, rms between back-to-back
   maps. The machine measures far better than it is built.

RULES
- Shims must span the FULL 12mm rail width. Washer-sized shims let the rail
  bridge and deform locally - defeats the point and wears the bearings.
- Grade it, never step it. Every fixing gets its own thickness.
- Fastener 1 gets nothing. All correction added going rearward.
- Do NOT shim the left rail. Do NOT shim the X beam (section 1b).
- Fasteners 1 and 10 are extrapolated beyond the probed Y16-380. #10 is the
  largest in the stack - check it with an indicator once the rail is back on.
- Re-measure everything after shimming; the shim changes what these describe.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. OPERATING PROCEDURE
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SOAK CRITERION - do NOT use frame temperature. temp0 flattens long before the
machine does; it read +0.03 C/min with the machine still 0.44mm from
equilibrium. The frame is only partly insulated, so different members have
different time constants and no single sensor can represent it.
Use the Z DATUM instead - it integrates the whole loop. Probe bed centre with
G30 S-1 every 3 min and wait for it to stop moving.
NOTE THE DATUM OVERSHOOTS AND COMES BACK: it peaked 10 min before reversing at
~2 microns/min during the 60C soak. Bed and Z system warm first (datum up), then
the towers catch up (datum down). A criterion that waits for "stopped changing"
will fire twice - once near the peak, once for real.

MESH: the ALPS cannot complete a large mesh in a hot chamber - it latches
"probe already triggered" after ~21-25 points. Known behaviour of this probe.
Use 4x4 hot. A cold 7x7 is fine.

SYNTHESISED HOT MESH: fine 7x7 cold, coarse 4x4 hot, subsample the cold map at
indices 0,2,4,6 in both axes (the 4x4 points are an exact subset), take the
difference, bilinear-interpolate up to 7x7, add to the cold map, recall with
G29 S1. Re-datum hot regardless - G29 S1 restores shape, not Z0.
The recalled map dies if the FR4 is lifted. Clip or magnetically hold it.

5. TRAPS - ALL OF THESE COST HOURS
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- M558 SILENTLY WIPES THE G31 TRIGGER HEIGHT. Any runtime M558 reset it from
  -0.030 to an old 0.700 placeholder while config.g still read -0.030. Every map
  taken in that window was displaced 0.73mm. ALWAYS re-issue G31 after M558, in
  that order. Verify: M409 K"sensors.probes[0]" -> triggerHeight must be -0.030.
- G30 S-1 reports the RAW trigger height; G29 stores it with G31 Z already
  subtracted. Comparing the two directly looks like a 0.7mm fault and is not one.
- RRF heightmap row 0 = Ymin = FRONT.
- Heightmap positive = bed reads HIGH = the gantry sits LOW there.
- bed.g's final centre G30 can fail silently and leave the datum unset, which
  displaces a whole map while reporting success. The result check is now in
  bed.g - keep it.
- DO NOT DRIVE THIS MACHINE WITH ONE "CodeConsole -c" PER COMMAND. Each
  invocation opens a fresh socket to DCS, allocates a code channel and tears it
  down. Dozens in tight loops correlate with the unexplained M112 halts, and the
  event log caught the mechanism: "Transfer timeout waiting for TfrRdy (waited
  504ms, 0 glitches)" - the Duet too busy to answer, not electrical noise.
  Halts only ever occurred during direct machine control, never in normal use.
  Batch commands down ONE connection instead:
      printf 'M114\nM409 K"state.status"\n' | CodeConsole
  SbcTransferTimeout also raised 500 -> 2000ms in /opt/dsf/conf/config.json
  (bak: config.json.bak-timeout). Needs a duetcontrolserver restart, not M999.
- M929 event logging is now enabled (eventlog.txt, on the SD card, survives
  reboots). Added after repeated unexplained M112 halts with no persistent log.
- Rebooting the Pi does NOT reset the Duet - it has its own power, and config.g
  does not re-run. Use M999.
- chamber_abort.g sets chamberSP=0, which makes chamber_heat.g's wait condition
  immediately false, so it exits the normal way and never clears chamberAbort.
  Still unfixed.
- daemon.g line 49: `if { var.plcRegs != null }` throws "unknown variable" when
  the Modbus read fails, aborting the file. Needs exists() guarding it.
